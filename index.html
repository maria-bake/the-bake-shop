How the pricing works

  • The listed price is the starting price for that cake in that size and flavour, with her usual buttercream finish, a piped border and a short message on top.
  • Costs a little more: heavy Lambeth piping, sculpted or themed designs, extra tiers, fresh flowers, gold plaques and bought toppers.
  • Priced on request: cheesecake cups, dessert boxes, cake bouquets and anything custom. Ask her for photos of what she has made before, and she will quote it in the chat. Cheesecake cups have a minimum order of 2.
  • Nothing is charged on this website. Mariya confirms the final price with you on WhatsApp or Instagram before she starts baking, so there are no surprises.
  • Sizes: bento serves 1–2, half kg serves 4–6, one kg serves 8–12. Bigger than 1 kg and tiered cakes are quoted separately.
  • Red velvet comes with cream cheese frosting instead of buttercream, and is made from half a kilo upwards.

About the baker

The person behind the bake shop

Mariya is a home baker in Howly, Barpeta. There is no big kitchen behind _thebakeshop._, no team and no shop. There is one oven, one piping bag, and one person who takes every order herself. She bakes in small batches so nothing sits waiting. Vanilla, butterscotch, strawberry, mango, chocolate, Biscoff, red velvet. Each cake leaves her kitchen fresh, on the day it is meant for.

Her style is soft and pastel. Buttercream in gentle colours, hand-piped Lambeth borders, ribbons, edible pearls and piped florals. She has made doctor-themed cakes, baby shower cakes, anniversary cakes and heart-shaped ones, and she enjoys a new idea. When a cake is ready she boxes it carefully and it is collected the same day, so it reaches you exactly as it left her table.

“I bake one cake at a time, and every one leaves my kitchen fresh.”

Good to know

Questions people actually ask

How much notice do you need for an order?
The sooner the better. Everything is baked to order by one person, so only a few cakes fit into a day. Message her with your date and she will tell you straight away if she can take it. For tiered or heavily decorated cakes, ask early.
Do you deliver?
No, there is no delivery. Orders are collected once they are ready. Message Mariya with your date, and she will tell you in the chat when your cake will be boxed and where you can pick it up.
Can I get a custom or themed cake?
Yes, that is most of what she does. Doctor-themed, baby shower, anniversary, heart-shaped, candle cakes, cake bouquets, dessert boxes. Send the idea, the colours and the occasion, and she will tell you what she can do in her own piping style.
What do the prices on this page include?
A price shown next to a cake, for example ₹530, is the starting price for that cake in that size and flavour. Extra tiers, a bigger size, heavy piping or a special theme can change it. She always confirms the final price in the chat before baking.
